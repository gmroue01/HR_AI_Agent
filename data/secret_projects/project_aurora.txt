Description:
Project Aurora is a research and development effort focused on prototyping a
machine-learning-based demand forecasting tool for the sales department. The
current phase involves training and validating models against two years of
historical sales data, with an emphasis on improving forecast accuracy during
seasonal peaks. Early results have shown a measurable reduction in forecast
error compared to the existing statistical baseline.
